Overview

A fast-paced 2D RTS about capturing planets, building an economy, and producing a fleet capable of overwhelming the opponent.

As technical and design lead, I guided the development of Inkvasion from engine architecture through moment-to-moment play. The game was written in C++ and built around a custom Entity Component System and data pipeline.

What I did

  • Designed the engine architecture and implemented its Entity Component System
  • Co-wrote the data serialization and deserialization pipeline
  • Designed gameplay flow, unit behaviours, controls, player interactions, and AI
  • Coordinated with the art and graphics team on assets and effects

Outcome

A complete real-time strategy loop supported by a purpose-built engine, data-driven content, and a playable AI opponent.
